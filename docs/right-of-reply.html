How a model's lab can answer a result, and what we do with the answer.

Right of reply

Every model in the lineup is a named product of a named lab, and every number about it is published. Its lab can answer.

What a lab can send#

  • a statement about a result, a case file or the methodology, published unedited next to the model's record;
  • a correction of a version string, a release date or a description of the model;
  • a request to retire a version from the lineup (the record of the runs already made stays published).

How#

Open an issue in the public repository with the label right-of-reply, or write from the lab's domain to the contact listed in the repository's SECURITY file. We answer and publish the reply as received.

What we do not do#

We do not remove runs, rerun a model until it looks better, or test private variants. A reply sits next to the data; it does not replace it.
