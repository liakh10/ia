Threat model: the verifier (plant registry, transparency log, replay kit)

Scope: ia/registry/, ia/log/, ia/replay/, ia/api/plant/*, ia/api/log/* (CONTRACT §7-§8). The program, the runner and the adapters have their own models (ia/audit/); where a threat crosses a boundary it is listed here with the owner of the other half. Every mitigation names the test that proves it (registry/test/*.test.mjs, registry/test/e2e/local-e2e.mjs).

Trust points (stated first, as on /docs)#

  1. The registry operator (us) could leak a plant before the run. A leak lets someone copy a plant; it cannot change one (the hash is on chain at plant time). Bounded by: texts are sealed at rest with AES-256-GCM, never logged, never returned by any public route before RoundSettled; the runner gets them only after plant close, through a signed request.
  2. The registry operator could refuse to deliver a paid plant. Bounded by: the delivery rule is mechanical (a quote with that hash, class, target, canary and, for {{PLANT}}, index), the MANIFEST leaf with every plant's decision is logged and anchored before the first run, and the program clears undelivered plants from the taken masks (withhold_plants) so the runner cannot quietly drop a plant and still settle it as "not taken" without the public seeing it. The replay kit checks Round.withheld = the manifest's undelivered set. Residual: a planter whose plant was wrongly marked undelivered has no on-chain remedy; the evidence (quote receipt with textHash, the manifest entry) is public, and the fee was split like any other plant's.
  3. The runner relays model outputs faithfully (SPEC trust point 1; owner: runner). The verifier narrows it: every act's step must be the hash of a tool_call entry in the committed transcript with the same arguments, the trail must recompute, every get_balance answer must equal the ledger at that moment, the inbox must equal runner/task byte for byte, and the open-weights model's every call must replay bit for bit on a batch-invariant endpoint. A forged closed-API answer that is self-consistent is still undetectable; rates over N runs (replay rates, replay env) are the only check there.
  4. The log operator (us) could show different histories to different people. Bounded by signed heads, consistency proofs, memo anchors that fix a head to a slot, and witnesses that cosign only extensions of what they saw (cosignHead).
