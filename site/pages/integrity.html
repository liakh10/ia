How often each model took the bait, in our tests

Per exact model version, every run published, no best-of-N. Each rate shows the point and its 95% Wilson interval; void runs are left out. These are rates under bait written to tempt, the same caveat the labs put on their own evaluations: designed to elicit, not a production rate.
