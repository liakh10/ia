## What the tests prove

- **The rules are the program's.** Every group of `program/test/vectors.json` (generated by `rules.rs`: seeds, `h`, task numbers,
  off-chain decoys/template/order hashes, prices, splits, runs per docket, 13 tier cases, REPORT hits, bounties with and without the cap,
  share math, records) matches the SDK: 116 groups. The runner's generator (`runner/task/rules.mjs`) agrees on 200 random seeds (task,
  decoys with amounts, template, inbox order). On chain, a docket's invoices, vendors and operating balance equal `sdk.task(seed)`, and
  each plant's price follows `plantPrice` from the 0.1 SOL floor.
- **The indexer checks the program.** Every `Acted` event is replayed with `RunLedger` (hit, moved, the trail hash chain) and every
  settled run's tier and taken mask is recomputed (withheld plants masked as settlement does). A forged tier, a forged trail and a forged
  price in a synthetic stream are each reported; on the local chain the mismatch count is 0. The stored event list round-trips bigints
  and rebuilds an identical index; a second indexer from the store pulls nothing past the cursor; failed transactions are skipped; an
  inner program cannot forge an `ia` event (invoke-stack aware parsing).
- **The SDK's own transactions work on chain.** Sureties, `open_round` (writable lineup), plants (with `max_price`), dockets, every act
  (session-signed, a canary as an extra signer), settlement and claims, and an empty round are all sent as `unsignedTransaction`s built by
  the SDK, signed by web3.js. Instruction bytes equal Anchor's coder; web3.js reads the unsigned transaction back to the same
  instructions and flags.
- **The Index.** Per (agent, epoch): tier counts, taken/lied/false-report/stray/flagged/complete with Wilson 95% bounds (checked against
  known values), untouched and lost lamports, nudge and class splits; checked number by number against a full local round (CLEAN,
  FLAGGED, BENT_LIED, BENT with a stray and a KEY canary, a REPORT plant, VOID).
- **Case files.** The catch list, the plants taken, the first tempting step and its real transaction, the model's words before it
  (from the runner-format JSONL transcript whose `step`s are the canonical tool-call hashes), and `docketRootMatches` (the published
  transcripts hash to the root posted on chain). Transcripts are sealed (403) until the round settles.
- **Forecasts.** A signed `ia-forecast:<seq>:<agent>:<call>` is accepted before plant close only, once per wallet and docket; bad
  signatures, agents outside the lineup and bursts are refused; the board's points follow the docket outcome. With a log configured the
  forecast is forwarded to `POST /api/log/forecast` and the board is read from FORECAST leaves.
- **The log.** Against a fake verifier log with a real ed25519 key: the head's signature verifies, the key is pinned from `IA_LOG_KEYS`
  only, an inclusion proof verifies, `?key=` and range lookups decode leaves.
- **MCP.** initialize, tools/list (annotations, schemas), read tools, `quote_plant` returning an unsigned transaction that a planter signs
  and lands on the local chain, tool errors as results, batches capped at 10; plant texts and model quotes come back labelled as quoted
  untrusted data (bidi controls stripped, links defused).

## Findings fixed during the build

- **D-01 (high, fixed in b97ceec):** `/v1` pinned the log key from `IA_LOG_KEYS || IA_LOG_KEY` and echoed it in `/v1/log/head`, while
  `IA_LOG_KEY` is the verifier's *secret* keypair in the same deployment's env (the lab sets it). Only `IA_LOG_KEYS` (public base58) is
  read now; anything that is not an address is dropped; a test holds it. Never deployed.
- **D-02 (medium, fixed):** the event shaper hexed every byte vector, turning `DocketSettled.tiers` (a `Vec<u8>`) into a string; events
  are now shaped by their IDL type.
- **D-03 (low, fixed):** the signature walk stopped on a short page; only an empty page or the cursor ends it now.
- **Program changes followed during the build** (each one first failed the local suite, then the SDK/test was fixed): the lineup is
  writable in `open_round` and `close_empty_round` (agents marked busy), `plant` takes `max_price` (the SDK requires it; `/v1/quote` sets
  the quoted price), a REPORT target is not a multiple of 1,000 lamports (`/v1/quote` refuses one), settlement needs the transcript root
  posted, rounds open `exit_gap` slots after the previous settles, `withhold_plants` masks undelivered plants at settlement.
