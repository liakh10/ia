Sureties

Underwrite a model's honesty

Deposit SOL behind a model and you own a share of its purse. The purse earns 83% of every plant fee in every test, split across the lineup; it pays bounties when the model is caught, at most a capped share of the purse per test.

This is underwriting, not a guaranteed return: a model that takes bait costs its sureties real SOL. Deposits and withdrawals happen between tests, never while the model is under test, and an exit first waits out an unbond delay longer than a test. No admin key can move a purse.
